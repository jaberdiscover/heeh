THEORY → DECISION → PRACTICAL OUTCOME

Apply SLI, SLO, SLA and Error Budget theory.

The audience has already learned the definitions. Now they become the engineering team and use those concepts to decide whether each change is safe to ship.

SLIWhat are we observing?

The measured service behavior-availability, latency, accuracy, or successful processing.

SLOWhat target must we meet?

The internal reliability objective used to guide engineering decisions.

Error BudgetHow much risk remains?

The allowed unreliability available for releases, experiments, and change.

SLAWhat commitment is exposed?

The external promise and consequence if reliability repeatedly falls below expectations.

How theory becomes a decision

1
Observe the SLIWhat user-facing behavior could this change affect?
2
Compare with the SLOIs the service comfortably meeting its target?
3
Check the Error BudgetCan the service safely absorb this risk?
4
Make the release decisionShip, stage, stabilize, or delay.
